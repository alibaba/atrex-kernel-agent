## Direction records

Direction recording is enabled; Experiment recording is disabled. Read `PROGRESS.md` and only the relevant `directions/<status>/*.json`. Reuse a Direction while its objective and applicability are unchanged, and check for duplicates before proposing another. A Direction is a durable optimization program around a bottleneck, workload regime, or architecture objective and should span multiple candidates or probes. A single implementation, parameter choice, capability probe, or mechanism test remains a bounded step inside an existing Direction; do not create a Direction for it. Runtime maintenance and infrastructure work are not Directions. Genealogy is descriptive and does not inherit source, evidence, status, or priority.

Lifecycle is explicit: `propose` creates `proposed`; call `start` to enter `in_progress`; only an `in_progress` Direction may `complete`, `abandon`, `defer`, or `block`. Restart `deferred` or `blocked` work with `start`. `completed` and `abandoned` are terminal, so continued work needs a derived Direction.

Multiple local Directions may be `in_progress`; keep that set intentional and update each Direction by ID. The most recently started active Direction is the focus; queue reordering does not change it. Choose executable work from the Local work queue; paused Directions are not next work until explicitly restarted. The Shared direction pool is discovery-only: absorb compatible peer ideas into one local derivative citing every source ID. Experiment recording is disabled, so omit `supporting_experiment_ids`; closure evidence uses `gateway_record_ids`. Follow the installed Skill for exact mutation fields and the Handoff section for report gates.

`propose` and revisions made while still proposed are unreviewed drafts: they are archived locally with `claim_review_quality=not_reviewed` and are not published to peers. `start` performs the first independent Review before work begins; revising an active Direction and every closure reviews the updated decision. A partial or reviewer-unavailable start may proceed locally, but `claim_review_quality=unreviewed` is not eligible for terminal evidence or peer publication. A closure requires a `supported` Review of whether the optimization program is genuinely exhausted or its lifecycle decision is justified. Unsupported starts, active revisions, and closures are rejected before mutation and return no `archive_path`. Runtime returns the full Review once; afterward `claim_review_quality`, `scope_safe_interpretation`, and `scientific_next_check` remain visible, while full receipts and reviewer conversations are Supervisor-private. Follow `runtime_action`; `scientific_next_check` is the evidence follow-up. Restart paused Directions first and continue terminal work through a derived Direction.
