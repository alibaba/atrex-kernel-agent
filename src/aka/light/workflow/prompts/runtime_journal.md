## Handoff

Direction and Experiment recording are enabled. Follow `skills/runtime-records/SKILL.md` for the exact schema. For `candidate_ready`, select a visible Experiment with a `supported` Review under the Experiment decision standard and a passing standard full Evaluate of the exact clean HEAD; a historical Experiment may be reused when its evidence still matches. Omit direct `gateway_record_ids`. `direction_terminal` is the ordinary handoff value and may preserve local `in_progress` Directions; the selected Experiment may belong to an `in_progress`, completed, or abandoned visible Direction. Pivot and blocked reports omit `selected_experiment_id`; a blocked handoff still requires every local active Direction to be blocked. Pooled `cross_trajectory_share` is reserved for publishing a reusable measured checkpoint and requires the selected Experiment to belong to an `in_progress` Direction.
