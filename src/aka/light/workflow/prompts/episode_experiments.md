## Experiment records

Experiment recording is enabled; Direction recording is disabled. Read only relevant `research-library/experiments/<experiment_id>/vN/experiment.json` files; the greatest visible `vN` is the current version. Revise only `local_writable` Experiments; extend `peer_read_only` evidence with a new local Experiment. Record each decisive measurement or research conclusion with `name`, a concrete `intent`, and a bounded `interpretation`; omit `direction_id`. `gateway_record_ids` and `references` are independent optional lists. Runtime validates cited Records and archives referenced workspace material beside that immutable version. Keep measured facts in `records/` and source facts in Git rather than restating them. Promotion evidence requires a passing standard full Evaluate of the exact current commit.

Records cited by an Experiment currently visible to this lineage and passing standard full-Evaluate Records are visible here; accepted submissions and winners therefore carry their complete Evaluate evidence. Other local Records remain private.

Each submission returns an inline independent `review`. `archive_status`, `claim_review_quality`, and `evidence_eligible` distinguish storage from scientific support; `runtime_action` describes workflow repair and `scientific_next_check` describes the evidence follow-up. Every archived Experiment revision enters `research-library/` with its actual review quality. Synchronous peers see the revision frozen at the last Epoch boundary; asynchronous peers see revisions immediately. A peer Experiment is read-only; record a new local Experiment to extend or correct it. Partial and `review_unavailable` Experiments remain visible but cannot support a research conclusion; unsupported submissions are not archived and have no `archive_path`. Runtime retries an unavailable Reviewer once before archiving with `claim_review_quality=review_unavailable`. Runtime returns the full Review once, while the projected Experiment keeps `claim_review_quality`, `scope_safe_interpretation`, and `scientific_next_check`; full receipts and reviewer conversations are Supervisor-private. Do not replay an identical mutation; revise a local Experiment with its stable `experiment_id`.
