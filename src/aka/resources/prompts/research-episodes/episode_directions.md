## Direction records

Direction recording is enabled; Experiment recording is disabled. Read `PROGRESS.md` and only relevant `research-library/directions/<direction_id>/vN/direction.json` files; the greatest visible `vN` is the current version. Reuse a Direction while its objective and applicability are unchanged, and check for duplicates before proposing another. A Direction is a durable optimization program around a bottleneck, workload regime, or architecture objective and should span multiple candidates or probes. A single implementation, parameter choice, capability probe, or mechanism test remains a bounded step inside an existing Direction; do not create a Direction for it. Runtime maintenance and infrastructure work are not Directions. Genealogy is descriptive and does not inherit source, evidence, status, or priority.

Lifecycle is explicit: `propose` creates `proposed`; call `start` to enter `in_progress`; only an `in_progress` Direction may `complete`, `abandon`, `defer`, or `block`. Restart `deferred` or `blocked` work with `start`. `completed` and `abandoned` are terminal, so continued work needs a derived Direction.

Multiple local Directions may be `in_progress`; keep that set intentional and update each Direction by ID. The most recently started active Direction is the focus; queue reordering does not change it. Choose executable work from the Local work queue; paused Directions are not next work until explicitly restarted. The Shared direction pool is discovery-only: absorb compatible peer ideas into one local derivative citing every source ID. Experiment recording is disabled, so omit `supporting_experiment_ids`; closure evidence uses `gateway_record_ids`. Follow the installed Skill for exact mutation fields.

Records cited by a Direction currently visible to this lineage and passing standard full-Evaluate Records are visible here; accepted submissions and winners therefore carry their complete Evaluate evidence. Other local Records remain private.

`propose` runs independent admission Review and writes immutable `v1`; no prior measurement is required. Every `revise` and closure is reviewed. `start` changes lifecycle without Review and retains the prior review quality. Each archived update writes the next immutable `vN`. Accepted versions enter the Campaign Research Library and are copied into each workspace according to its visibility boundary. Synchronous peers see the version frozen at the last Epoch boundary; asynchronous peers see the latest version immediately. Peer identities are read-only and must be continued through a new local derived Direction. Review labels evidence strength, not visibility. A partial or `claim_review_quality=review_unavailable` state may proceed but is not established support for a research conclusion; Runtime retries an unavailable Reviewer once before using that state. A closure requires a `supported` Review of whether the optimization program is genuinely exhausted or its lifecycle decision is justified. Unsupported proposals, revisions, and closures are rejected before mutation and return no `archive_path`, so there is no record to publish. Runtime returns the full Review once; afterward `claim_review_quality`, `scope_safe_interpretation`, and `scientific_next_check` remain visible, while full receipts and reviewer conversations are Supervisor-private. Follow `runtime_action`; `scientific_next_check` is the evidence follow-up. Restart paused Directions first and continue terminal work through a derived Direction.
